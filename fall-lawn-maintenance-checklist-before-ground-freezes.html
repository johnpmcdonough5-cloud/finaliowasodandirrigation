Fall Lawn Maintenance Checklist Before the Ground Freezes

A few fall steps keep your turf strong through the cold months and ready to green up fast in spring.

Fall lawn care isn't complicated, but it is time-sensitive. Everything on this list works better before the ground freezes than after.

Mowing Height Going Into Winter

Grass that's too long going into winter is more prone to matting down under snow, which can invite disease in spring. Grass cut too short loses the leaf area it needs to keep photosynthesizing in the cooler fall weeks. A moderate height for the last few mows of the season gives the lawn the best position heading into dormancy.

Clearing Debris Before It Sits All Winter

Leaves and other debris left on the lawn through winter block light and trap moisture against the grass, which can lead to matted, damaged patches once snow melts in spring. Clearing the yard before the ground freezes is far easier than dealing with it after a snow cover has set in.

Checking the Irrigation System Separately

Lawn maintenance and irrigation winterization are two different jobs on two different timelines. The blowout needs to happen before the first hard freeze, which typically arrives early to mid October in eastern Iowa. If that hasn't been scheduled yet, it belongs at the top of the list, ahead of any of the general lawn tasks.

What a Maintenance Visit Covers

A fall maintenance visit is built around exactly this kind of seasonal prep work, getting the lawn into good shape before winter dormancy rather than trying to fix problems after they've already set in over the cold months.

Common questions

Should I mow shorter or longer for the last cuts of the season?

A moderate height works best. Too long risks matting under snow, too short removes leaf area the grass still needs before dormancy.

Do I need to clear leaves off the lawn before winter?

Yes. Leaves left over winter block light and trap moisture, which can damage the grass underneath by the time snow melts.

Is irrigation winterization part of fall lawn maintenance?

They're related but separate jobs. The sprinkler blowout has its own hard deadline tied to the first freeze and shouldn't wait on general lawn cleanup.
