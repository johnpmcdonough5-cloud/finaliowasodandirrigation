Is There Still Time for Sprinkler Winterization Before Thanksgiving?

A few fall steps keep your turf strong through the cold months and ready to green up fast in spring.

Thanksgiving week is a natural checkpoint for anyone who's been putting off winterizing their sprinkler system. Here's where things actually stand at this point in the season.

The Freeze Deadline Has Likely Already Passed

Eastern Iowa's first hard freeze typically arrives early to mid October, well before Thanksgiving in most years. If your system isn't winterized yet, there's a real chance a freeze has already reached it, whether or not any damage has shown up yet.

Why Getting It Done Now Still Matters

Even after an initial freeze, winter has months left to go, with more freezes still ahead. Getting the blowout done now, even if it's later than ideal, still prevents additional damage from every freeze between now and spring.

What to Expect If Damage Already Happened

If a freeze already reached water in the system, a blowout at this point won't undo any existing damage, but it will stop the situation from getting worse. Any repairs from a prior freeze typically aren't discovered until the system is run again in spring.

Don't Let the Timing Talk You Out of It

It's tempting to think that if you've already missed the ideal window, there's no point rushing now. That's backwards. The later it is, the more urgent it becomes to get the water out before the next freeze adds to the damage.

Common questions

Has the first freeze definitely already happened by Thanksgiving?

In most years, yes, since eastern Iowa's first hard freeze typically lands early to mid October. It's worth assuming it has and acting accordingly.

Is it still worth winterizing if a freeze already happened?

Yes. It won't undo existing damage, but it prevents further freezes over the rest of winter from making things worse.

How would I know if damage already occurred?

Usually you wouldn't know until the system runs again in spring. That uncertainty is a reason to get the blowout done as soon as possible rather than waiting.
